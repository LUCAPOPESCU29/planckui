# Prooflet — Design Context (via impeccable teach protocol; pre-answered in build brief)

## Users

Solo founders, small e-commerce brands, creators, and agencies embedding widgets for
clients. Context: setting up a website quickly, often non-technical, laptop or phone,
daytime. Jobs: "make my site look trustworthy in 5 minutes", "collect testimonials without
paying $25/mo", "embed one widget and forget it."

## Brand Personality

Quiet, precise, dependable. Calm confidence, zero hustle; trust through restraint. The
product should feel like a well-made physical tool. Emotional goals: confidence, calm.

## Aesthetic Direction

Light-first (SMB daytime context) with flawless dark mode, including inside embeds.
Engineered-tool aesthetic: sturdy grotesque display (Archivo), calm even body (Golos Text),
OKLCH palette tinted toward a single deep petrol hue, rare accent, generous whitespace.
Anti-references: Elfsight's upsell-heavy cluttered dashboard, generic admin SaaS, AI-slop
gradients, cyan-on-dark glow, badge-icon-above-heading templates.

## Design Principles

1. The next action is always visible and plain-spoken; no dead ends.
2. Restraint is the brand: one accent, few sizes, space over decoration.
3. Previews are real: every editor change updates a true render instantly.
4. Motion only where it explains state; under 300ms; reduced-motion is first-class.
5. Embeds must never slow a host site down (tiny script, zero layout shift).
